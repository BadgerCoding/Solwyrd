WHAT IS THIS?

PB's Scan and Fun turns studying into a turn-based RPG. Upload or scan a document, an AI reads it and builds a quiz, and every correct answer lands a hit on the enemy — just like a classic Pokémon-style battle.

SCAN / UPLOAD ➜ AI MAKES QUIZ ➜ PICK DIFFICULTY ➜ TURN-BASED BATTLE ➜ XP & REWARDS

GAME MODES

STORY MODE

Follow the main questline through PB Presents — level up your character and battle through a scripted RPG campaign.

CREATE YOUR OWN

Upload a document or scan a single page of notes. The AI reads it and builds a custom quiz-battle just for that material.

ONLINE

Host or join a lobby, pick a character and a map, and battle other players 1v1, 2 vs E, or in an 8-player tournament.

LAN

No internet? No problem. Play locally over Wi-Fi or hotspot with nearby classmates.
